Fedele’s Admin privacy policy

Last reviewed: 16 October 2026

Who and what this policy covers

Fedele’s at Dural Pty Ltd, trading as Fedele’s East Gosford, operates Fedele’s Admin for its authorised management team. This policy covers the Admin app and its management services. The restaurant’s public website and customer app may have separate practices.

Information we handle

  • Account identity, names, usernames or authorised email addresses, roles, authentication and device-session records.
  • Staff profiles, roster assignments and publication history, availability and shift-change requests, decisions and management notes.
  • Operational notification content, delivery status, device registration identifiers and push tokens.
  • Security and service records, such as access times, request metadata, errors and audit trails. Infrastructure request logs can include IP addresses.
  • Owner campaign drafts, review and delivery records, customer audience summaries and authorised test-device references. These tools are restricted to the Owner.

Notes and requests may contain personal or sensitive information, including health information voluntarily supplied by employees. Include only information necessary for the business purpose and restrict access appropriately. The app does not use HealthKit or collect Face ID biometric templates. Face ID and device authentication are handled by Apple’s operating system.

How information is used

Account and operational records support authentication, permissions, restaurant management, staff scheduling, request handling, operational notifications, security and troubleshooting. We do not use Admin staff records for advertising, cross-app advertising tracking, sale of personal information or unrelated organisations’ marketing.

The Owner’s separate campaign tools can send Fedele’s offers and news to eligible customer-app installations with the required consent. This does not mean employee roster or health information is used for marketing.

Access and service providers

Access depends on business authorisation and server-enforced permissions. Owner-only functions are restricted to the Owner. Services use Fly.io for the roster backend and database, Google Cloud for management services and Firestore, Google authentication for Owner sign-in, and Apple Push Notification service for native notifications. Operational web push, where enabled for related staff web clients, can use Apple, Google or Mozilla push infrastructure. The approved support mailbox uses Google Workspace.

Roster storage and management compute/database regions inspected are Sydney. This is not a promise that all processing occurs in Australia: infrastructure logs, provider support, authentication and notification delivery may involve overseas processing. Provider support and subprocessor locations and contractual deletion periods have not been fully verified. We do not promise that all processing stays in Australia or give a provider-wide final-erasure deadline.

These public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, embedded forms or external fonts.

Current retention and deletion

Operational records do not currently have a general automated deletion or anonymisation schedule. Deactivating an account, retiring a staff profile, signing out or dismissing a notification does not erase all associated historical records. Records may be retained for business, security and applicable employment-record obligations. We are reviewing a documented retention schedule; no new schedule is represented as implemented.

Record or copyVerified current behaviour
Roster records, notes, requests, audit and notification recordsNo general automatic purge identified in the deployed roster application. Individual workflows may update, revoke or delete particular records; this is not a complete account-erasure process.
Roster daily backupsThe latest 14 dated daily database backup files are retained. Named pre-change backups are outside that cleanup rule and currently have no automatic expiry.
Fly volume snapshotsAutomatic backups are enabled with 30-day snapshot retention. Snapshots include the database and backup files present on the volume.
Google Cloud logsStandard logs: 30 days. Required audit logs: 400 days.
Firestore management recordsNo configured automatic deletion rules or backup schedules; no existing backups returned in the inspected Sydney location. Point-in-time recovery is disabled; version retention is one hour.
Source/build storageNo lifecycle expiry found in the inspected bucket configuration. Deleted objects have a seven-day soft-delete window. Source archives are not intended to contain business database records.

Deletion from a live record does not immediately remove historical copies or backups. Named backups have no verified maximum retention, so we cannot promise a fixed final-erasure deadline. Project documentation and existing files verify that local off-host migration/maintenance backups and diagnostic exports also exist on the operator’s Mac. No automatic retention rule for those copies was verified. Any further synchronised, archived or exported copies remain unverified. Fly’s current documentation states seven days for searchable application logs; separately exported logs may have different retention.
