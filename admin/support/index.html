Fedele’s Admin support

Last reviewed: 16 October 2026

Help for the management team

Fedele’s Admin is the private workspace for the authorised Owner and Managers of Fedele’s East Gosford. An existing business-authorised account is required. Downloading the app does not grant access to restaurant records.

For sign-in, permissions, rosters, requests or app support, contact dominic@fedeleseastgosford.com.au.

What to include

Describe the screen and the problem, your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the app version and build shown in More. Share screenshots only after removing staff details and other sensitive information. Never send passwords or verification codes.

Sign-in and access

Use the sign-in method authorised for your role. If your account is unavailable or your access needs changing, contact the business Owner. Owner privileges are not included with a Manager account.

Face ID and saved sessions

Check that device authentication is configured in iPhone or iPad Settings. If a saved sign-in cannot be restored, use the app’s sign-in screen. Contact support if the problem continues.

Notifications

Open More in Admin and check the notification status. Enable notifications for the device when prompted, and check Settings → Notifications → Fedele’s Admin. Focus and notification settings can affect when alerts appear.

Privacy enquiries

Use the same support address for access, correction and deletion enquiries. See our Admin privacy policy.
