Fedele's East Gosford Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Fedele's East Gosford respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how Fedele's at Dural Pty Ltd (ABN 68 622 489 196), trading as Fedele's East Gosford ("Fedele's", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you use FedeleApp, our website, online ordering services or otherwise interact with us.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use FedeleApp and our services, we may collect:

  • your name;
  • mobile phone number;
  • email address;
  • order details, including items, modifiers, discounts and order totals;
  • selected pickup dates and times;
  • special instructions you choose to provide with an order;
  • transaction and payment status information;
  • order history;
  • favourites and other preferences stored by the app;
  • notification preferences and notification history;
  • device information and push-notification tokens required to deliver notifications;
  • information you provide when contacting us with an enquiry, request or complaint.

You should not include sensitive personal or health information in the Special Instructions field. If you have an allergy or important dietary requirement, please contact the restaurant directly.

Payments

Payments made through FedeleApp are processed using Square.

Fedele's does not receive or store your complete credit or debit card number through FedeleApp. Payment credentials, including Apple Pay payment credentials where applicable, are handled through the relevant payment services and Square's payment infrastructure.

We receive information necessary to process and manage your order, such as payment status, transaction references and order totals.

How we collect information

We generally collect information directly from you when you:

  • place an order through FedeleApp;
  • enter your contact or pickup details;
  • provide Special Instructions;
  • contact Fedele's;
  • interact with notifications or other FedeleApp features.

Some technical and transaction information is also received from service providers involved in operating FedeleApp, including Square and Apple.

How we use information

We may use your information to:

  • process and fulfil your orders;
  • arrange and manage pickup times;
  • process payments and discounts;
  • send order confirmations, receipts and tax invoices;
  • send notifications relating to your order;
  • display your previous orders and support reorder functionality;
  • operate favourites and other app features;
  • respond to enquiries or complaints;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot and improve FedeleApp and our ordering systems;
  • maintain business, taxation and transaction records where required;
  • prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorised transactions;
  • comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

Email and notifications

When you successfully place an order, we may send you an email confirming your order and providing your receipt or tax invoice.

Fedele's may also receive an order-notification email containing information required to prepare and fulfil your order.

If you allow push notifications, FedeleApp may send notifications about your order, including confirmation and pickup information.

With your permission where required, we may also use notifications to tell you about Fedele's news, menu updates, special offers or promotions.

You can control push-notification permissions through your iPhone settings.

FedeleApp may store notification history within the app so that you can review previous notifications.

Information stored on your device

FedeleApp may store information such as your Past Orders, favourites and notification history on your device.

FedeleApp currently does not require you to create or sign into a customer account. Past Orders and other locally stored customer information are therefore associated with that installation of FedeleApp rather than a Fedele's customer account.

As a result, locally stored information may not automatically transfer if you delete FedeleApp, reinstall it or change devices.

When you use the Reorder feature, FedeleApp checks your previous order against the current Square Online menu, availability and pricing before rebuilding your cart. Previous prices, unavailable items or unavailable modifiers are not automatically treated as current.

Special Instructions

FedeleApp allows you to provide optional Special Instructions with an order.

Special Instructions may be transmitted with your order to Square and may be displayed to Fedele's staff through restaurant order-management, kitchen-display or printing systems. They may also appear in order-confirmation communications.

Please do not use Special Instructions to provide sensitive personal information. For allergies or important dietary requirements, contact Fedele's directly.

Disclosure to service providers

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to providers that help us operate FedeleApp and fulfil your order, including:

  • Square, for ordering, payments, discounts, transaction processing and related restaurant operations;
  • Apple, where necessary to operate iOS features including Apple Pay and push notifications;
  • Google, including Google Cloud infrastructure used to operate FedeleApp and Google Workspace services used to deliver email communications;
  • hosting, technology and infrastructure providers used to operate FedeleApp;
  • professional advisers and service providers where reasonably required for our business;
  • government, regulatory or law-enforcement bodies where required or authorised by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where information is processed

FedeleApp's primary backend infrastructure and persistent application data are hosted using Google Cloud infrastructure located in Sydney, Australia.

Some third-party service providers used to operate FedeleApp, including payment, email, notification and technology providers, may process limited information using infrastructure located outside Australia.

Where required by applicable privacy laws, we take reasonable steps in relation to the handling of personal information by our service providers.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

FedeleApp uses technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. Sensitive server credentials used for services such as payment processing, email delivery and push notifications are not intended to be stored within the customer-facing iOS application.

No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to meet legal, accounting, taxation and business-record requirements. Information that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

Access and correction

You may contact us if you would like to request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete or misleading.

We may need to verify your identity before processing an access or correction request.

Because some FedeleApp information is stored only on your device, we may not hold a server-side copy of every item displayed within the app.

Questions and complaints

If you have a question or complaint about how we handle your personal information, please contact us using the details below.

We will review your enquiry or complaint and respond within a reasonable period.

If your concern is not resolved, you may have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Third-party services

FedeleApp may interact with or link to third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy information provided by those services where relevant.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FedeleApp, our business practices, service providers or legal obligations change.

The current version will be made available through FedeleApp and published on the Fedele's East Gosford website. The Last updated date will be revised when the policy changes.
